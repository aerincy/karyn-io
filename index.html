My work centers on turning complex stakeholder needs into organized, accountable execution. Since 2020, I have supported KSC and Space Coast projects involving government, defense, commercial-space, and customer-facing priorities.

Mission Continuity During a Critical Contract Transition

Action:
Provided interim program leadership during the four-month transition. Coordinated with client stakeholders, organized the operating team, maintained visibility across active work, and worked within a multi-tier agreement environment to support consistent service delivery.
Result:
Helped stabilize the transition, supported continuity of service, and transferred responsibilities to permanent leadership with clearer communication and operating discipline.
